# Power BD design system

The look of the Power BD app: Notion's calm workspace in Powerhouse's colours, with a light and a dark theme. Its palette, type and mark come from the Powerhouse brand system (`design-systems/powerhouse/`, in a separate repo); its layout and density follow Notion. The tokens, components, icons and status vocabulary here are the ones the app uses. The showcase page renders them all, in both themes, along with mock-ups of the main screens.

## See it

Open `design-system/index.html` in a browser, straight from disk. It also works at **http://localhost:2927/design-system/** while Vetra runs (keep the trailing slash: without it you get Connect).

The switch at the top offers four modes:
- **Side by side**: small demos show light and dark together, and wide demos and screens flip in place.
- **Light** and **Dark**.
- **Auto**: follows Connect's theme, or the system's when the page is opened as a file.

A link can pick the mode: `?mode=split|light|dark|connect`.

The page loads `showcase.js`, a bundle of `showcase.ts`, `icons.ts` and `vocabulary.ts`. After changing any of those three, run `bun run design`.

## Files

| File | What it is | Used by the app |
|---|---|---|
| `tokens.css` | Every colour, size, radius, shadow and timing, as `--pb-*` custom properties, with light and dark values. The palette is built on the Powerhouse brand tokens | Yes |
| `components.css` | The component classes, prefixed `.pb-*`. They read only tokens, so they follow the theme | Yes |
| `icons.ts` | 78 icons as one SVG sprite, with typed names (`IconName`) | Yes |
| `vocabulary.ts` | Every status, kind and signal the app shows, with its label, tone and icon | Yes |
| `index.html`, `showcase.ts`, `showcase.css` | The showcase page | No |
| `showcase.js` | Generated by `bun run design`. Do not edit | No |
| `COGNITIVE-AUDIT.md` | The Six Minds audit, the concepts weighed, the lexicon, and each screen's audit | It guides every screen |

## Themes

Light is the default. Dark applies under Connect's `.dark` class on the root element, or inside any element with `data-theme="dark"`. `data-theme="light"` forces light inside a dark page. The app therefore needs no theme code: Connect's own theme switch drives it.

## Rules

- **Use tokens, never raw values.** A colour or size that is not a token belongs in `tokens.css` first.
- **Powerhouse colours.**
  - **Neutrals:** slate.
  - **Dark surfaces:** Powerhouse's own (`#0E0E0D` page, `#1A1D22` cards).
  - **Primary actions:** charcoal (`--pb-primary`), near-white in dark.
  - **Blue (`--pb-accent`):** Powerhouse blue, for links, focus, selection and "in progress" only.
- **The gradient means the agent.** The Powerhouse cyan-to-purple gradient (`--pb-gradient`) marks only what the agent drafted or did (`.pb-agent`).
- **Inter only.** Connect self-hosts Inter, and the Powerhouse app templates use it for headings too. Poppins stays a marketing font.
- **Colour carries meaning.** Statuses take their tone from `vocabulary.ts`. The tones are built from the Powerhouse status colours (progress `#329DFF`, success `#4FC86F`, to-do `#FFA132`, destructive `#EA4335`, yellow `#FAC400`, purple `#8E55EA`), plus slate, brown and pink:
  - grey: not started or neutral
  - blue: in progress
  - purple: waiting on the other side
  - yellow: needs a decision
  - orange: needs attention
  - green: done well
  - red: failed or overdue
  - brown: paused

  Tags always carry their label, and signals their icon and reason, so nothing depends on colour alone.
- **No coloured edges.** No card, panel, table row or other component carries a coloured stripe along one side. A state shows as a signal line (`.pb-signal`), a tag or an icon.
- **Owner colours.** An owner's mark takes one of six tones (blue, green, purple, pink, brown, yellow) from the member's place on the team list. Red and orange are never an owner's colour: they mean slipping.
- **People-only actions** (approving or marking a message sent, approving positioning, answering a decision, go or no-go, marking a submission) carry the `people-only` mark (`.pb-btn__people`).
- **Confidential items** always carry the `lock` icon and the purple callout.
- **Contrast.** Body and secondary text, links and button labels meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both themes. Keep it that way when changing a token.
- **Invented data only** in the showcase (K1): no real organisations, people or assessments.

## Six minds

Screens are designed with the `six-minds-design` skill in `.claude/skills/six-minds-design/SKILL.md` (John Whalen's Six Minds of Experience). `COGNITIVE-AUDIT.md` holds the audit and the reasoning. These parts of the system exist because of it:

| Mind | Rule | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Vision | One anchor per screen. Slipping items can be found by scanning. Headline figures stay quiet | `.pb-attention`, red and orange `.pb-signal` lines, `.pb-tiles` (quiet summary tiles), `.pb-pulse` |
| Wayfinding | A record shows where it is on its journey, and opening one keeps the list in view. Time reads as distance | `.pb-path`, `.pb-peek`, breadcrumbs, `.pb-strip` (the days coming up, side by side); dialogs rise, the peek slides from the right, menus drop |
| Memory | Behave like Notion and CRMs: automatic saving that shows, ⌘K search, gear, bin, undo, filter pills that drop a menu | `.pb-save-state`, `.pb-search-trigger`, toasts with Undo, `.pb-menu` |
| Language | BD words, never system terms. Summaries first, detail one click deeper | the lexicon, `vocabulary.ts`, `.pb-disclosure` |
| Decision | Each warning names its fix. Prerequisites come before the choice, and help sits at the point of decision. A new space is set up in numbered steps, one short form at a time | `.pb-next`, `.pb-checks`, `.pb-hint`, `action` in `vocabulary.ts`, `.pb-guide`, `.pb-steps`, `.pb-field__help` with `.pb-eg` |
| Emotion | One reassurance line beside each risky step, and small rewards for progress. An empty place shows an example and the one button that starts it | `.pb-reassure`, `.pb-ring`, `.pb-celebrate`, the tick's pop, `.pb-empty--start` |

Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Moving a card on the board

A card moves to another stage by dragging or with its "Move to…" list (`.pb-card__move`, `.pb-card__moves`, `.pb-move`). In the hand it is `.is-lifted`, then `.is-away` where it was. A stage that takes it is `.is-target` (a tinted surface, deeper with `.is-over`); one that refuses it is `.is-refused`; `.pb-board__hint` is the one line that says what a drop does or why not; on a folded stage it stands inside the strip, set vertically like the stage's name, so it never covers the cards beside it. The board (`.pb-board--fit`) keeps room inside its scrolling edges for the halo of a stage and the shadow of the question. A move that needs an answer asks in the stage with `.pb-ask` (which status, why, or where the decision is taken), with a `.pb-reassure` line when it closes or parks. A refusal by the record is a `.pb-field__error` inside the card. No part has a coloured edge. What each move does comes from `editors/power-bd/kit/overview-view/move.ts`.

### Proposals

A lead's proposals show as cards right under its Next bar (`.pb-proposal-cards`, `.pb-proposal-card`: title, status tag, value, next action). Wherever a lead is listed, `.pb-proposal-badge` says it has a proposal and where that stands. A value that is not sent yet is a `.pb-draft-value` with its `.pb-draft-value__note` ("Draft"): quieter than a counted value, and added to no total. In the Proposals place, `.pb-waits` (`--ours`, `--theirs`, `--none`) says whose move a proposal is; an answer past its valid-until day is a red `.pb-signal`. `.pb-mark` marks a phrase inside running text; the marks of the "Coming up" strip are `.pb-strip__mark`.

### Proposal page

The proposal page has two views, never side by side, chosen in a `.pb-viewbar` with a `.pb-segmented--lg` switch: Edit (tabs at full width) and Preview. Above both: `.pb-backlinks` (the lead and the organisation, as `.pb-backlink` buttons), the path, the Next bar, one `.pb-actions` panel (`__row`, `__do`, `__do--wide`, `__end`, `__fact`; each risky step with its `.pb-reassure`) and `.pb-findings` (a `details` whose rows are `.pb-finding` buttons naming where to fix them; the place they lead to gets `.is-found` once). Form cells size with `.pb-cell--text`, `--short`, `--area` and `--fill`. The Preview is a `.pb-doc`, a page the width of the column whose margins shrink with it. A second, small `.pb-segmented` in the view bar shows it as the Document or as Markdown (`.pb-doc__source`: the text Export saves, kept as written). Beside Export, Copy markdown is a `.pb-btn--swap` (two labels in one place, the other `.is-off`, so "Copied" keeps the button's width), and a refused copy is a `.pb-field__error` on a line of its own under the buttons. The page holds `.pb-doc__title`, `.pb-doc__fields` (a `dd.is-blank` is a line to sign), `.pb-doc__toc`, `.pb-doc-table` (`.is-num`, `.is-code`, rows `.is-group` and `.is-total`), `.pb-doc__ticks` with `.pb-doc__box`, `.pb-doc__figure`, and `.pb-doc__gap` on text still to be filled in. Tabs never wrap.

### Chat

One conversation, two presentations, switched from the conversation's header. The **full view** is the Agent place: `.pb-chat-view`, two panes (`__main` with `__head`, `__ready` and `__model`, `__body`; `__side` with `__side-head` and `.pb-chat-setup`), in `.pb-scroll--fill` so the page doesn't scroll and the messages do. It follows its own width (a container query): where two panes don't fit, `__side` becomes a sheet (`.is-open`) opened from `__more` (⋯), with `__side-close`, and "No key yet" (`__empty`) shows `__add-key`. Set-up holds the key card and `.pb-chat-setup__card`s (`__title`, `__reads`): what the agent reads and the set-up steps left. The **side panel** is `.pb-chat`, docked as a third column of `.pb-app--chat` (`__head`, `__title`, `__head-end`, `__reads`, `__body`); below 1200px it floats over the place like the peek. "Open beside" (`peek` icon) and "Open full view" (`expand`) are the switch; ⋯ opens Set-up in either. "Ask the agent" (`.pb-chat-entry`, `aria-pressed` while the chat shows) sits at the end of the top bar; below 1100px it keeps only its mark, and the crumbs keep a minimum so the search gives way first. The Shell holds the conversation: each presentation shows its held DOM, `.pb-chat__held` (the stack's `ChatPanel`, styled through its `ph-chat-*` classes with tokens only, with `.pb-chat__welcome` as its empty state and `.pb-chat__foot` under the composer) in a `.pb-chat__slot`, and Clear in a `.pb-chat__slot-clear`. Before the conversation, `.pb-chat__state` shows what is needed as `.pb-checks` and, for an admin, the key card `.pb-key` (`__head`, `__text`, `__form`, `__set` with the masked hint, `__actions`, `__confirm` for removal, and once a key is set `__auto`: the Auto-approve `.pb-switch`, each person's own), each risky step with its `.pb-reassure`. Where the server doesn't run the agent for the person, their own OpenRouter key stands in on the same card: "Your OpenRouter key", "in this browser" in `__set`, Change model, and `.pb-key__note` for the plain trade-off (a server key is safer, said with a neutral icon); the full view's `__empty` becomes "Use your own key in this browser", and the side panel shows the checks, then the card. Every write, the eight steps that are a person's included, waits on the stack's approval card unless Auto-approve is on; the opening line and `.pb-chat__foot` say which. Only the agent's mark carries the gradient; no part has a coloured edge, and a quote in an answer is a tint. The rules the agent works under (confidential items withheld, where the vault's tools read, its instructions, each person's choices) live in `editors/power-bd/kit/chat/`.

### Vault notes

A note linked from BD shows the vault's title today: as a list row (`.pb-note`, its title `.pb-note__title` the link out, with `.pb-note__open` saying it leaves for the vault) or as a chip (`.pb-ref` with `.pb-ref__title`). While the vault is asked, the title it was linked with shows over `.pb-note__wait`. One that can't be read is `.is-unavailable`: that title in grey and the reason in a few words (`.pb-note__why`, `.pb-ref__why`), never red. Notes are added with `.pb-picker`: a search field whose results (`.pb-picker__option` with `__title`, `__desc` and `__kind`) drop over the page in a `.pb-popover`; the filled row is where Enter goes, a note already linked is `.is-linked`, and a sticky `__foot` holds the reassurance and the way to type an id instead (`__switch`). `__status` says the vault is being asked or nothing matched; `__typed` with `__why` is the fallback when search can't run. ⌘K lists notes in a group of their own, "Notes in the vault". BD only reads the vault, and no part has a coloured edge.

### Access

Settings → Access (`.pb-access`) is a `.pb-list` of the drive's grants, each row with a level `.pb-select` (View, Edit, Manage) and a remove button; `.pb-access__confirm` holds the one confirm a change or removal waits for, and `.pb-access__add` is the add row (address, level, "Give access"). `.pb-access__note` says when the server keeps no permission tables. The sign-in gate is `.pb-gate` with one `.pb-gate__card`: an icon, `.pb-gate__title`, a line, and either the sign-in action or `.pb-gate__address` with copy. Demo: `#access`.

### A list that scrolls

`.pb-list--scroll` caps a `.pb-list` at 400 px (or 55 % of the window, if less) and scrolls it inside its section, so what follows stays in view (Coming up on the Overview). Its `.pb-list__group` headings stay pinned while it scrolls, and a soft shade along the bottom says there is more below; the shade goes at the end. Give it `tabindex="0"`, `role="region"` and a name, so the keyboard scrolls it too.

### Today in the strip

The strip's first day is today. Its date sits in a filled circle in the primary colour, and the cell carries `aria-current="date"`. Every date sits in the same 22 px round box, so the marks below stay in line. The app rolls the strip over at midnight in the team's time zone by itself.

### A date that has passed

A passed date reads in red with its age: `.pb-relative.is-overdue`, "Tue 10 Nov · 7 days overdue", and "1 day overdue" for one day. A card row has no room for that: there the date stays plain and whole (`.pb-card__date`), and the card ends with the slipping signal (`.pb-signal`, "Next step overdue · 7 days"), as the Overview's cards do.

### A place while its records first load

The frame (sidebar, top bar) paints at once. The place's pane shows its title (`.pb-page__title`) and four `.pb-skeleton` lines in a `.pb-stack.pb-gap-12` with `role="status"`, then the place fills in, with the sidebar's counts, in one go. It shows only the first time a drive's records load in a page; going back to a place, or to the app, paints at once. Until the records are known a place is loading, never empty: no empty state or set-up message stands in for records that have not arrived.

## Using it in the app

1. Import `tokens.css` and `components.css` once, in the BD drive app. Don't load a font: Connect already provides Inter.
2. Put `.pb-root` on the app's outermost element.
3. Insert the sprite once (`spriteMarkup()` from `icons.ts`), and render each icon as `<svg class="pb-icon"><use href="#pb-i-NAME"/></svg>`. A small `Icon` component that takes an `IconName` keeps the names type-checked.
4. Take every status label and tone from `vocabulary.ts`.

New UI parts are added here first, with a demo on the showcase page, and then used in the app.

## App icon

`app-icon.svg` is the drive tile's icon in Connect: an arrow in the bullseye, the deal won. It is drawn from this design system, and a test fails if it uses a colour that is not a token:

- **Tile:** the charcoal primary, `--pb-primary` (`#32373b`) to `--pb-primary-pressed` (`#181b1d`), with corners in the Powerhouse mark's proportion (6.3 of 47.25, so 68 of 512).
- **Target:** the `leads` icon's rings, on the icons' 24-unit grid with 1.5 strokes and round caps, in the dark theme's `--pb-text` (`#f3f5f7`): the tile is a dark surface.
- **Arrow:** the dark theme's `--pb-accent-text` (`#329dff`), Powerhouse blue, the colour of focus: a head in the bullseye drawn like the `investors` icon's arrow, and two chevrons of fletching, in a 1-unit line, finer than the rings, lying on top of them. The gradient stays the agent's.

Its charcoal and line drawing keep it apart from the knowledge vault's black glass prism and Sektar's green leaves. Every version carries `id="power-bd-app-icon"`, so the app replaces an earlier version of its own icon but never an icon someone chose. Connect reads a drive's tile from the drive's own `icon`, so the app sets it on open when the drive has none (`editors/power-bd/kit/app-icon.ts`, which a test keeps identical to this file).
